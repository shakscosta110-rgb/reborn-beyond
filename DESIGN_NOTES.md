# Reborn Beyond - Design Notes

A BitLife-style isekai life sim, built as a free browser game for phones. Original characters, creatures, worlds and story. Inspired by genre tropes, not copied from any one series.

## Hosting
- Code: this GitHub repo (shakscosta110-rgb/reborn-beyond)
- Live site: reborn-beyond-game.netlify.app (Netlify free plan, updated by dropping a fresh ZIP in the Deploys tab)
- Everything must stay free. Netlify free plan has a hard monthly credit limit.

## Built so far
- Title screen sliced diagonally, then male or female choice
- Random death scenes (truck, stabbing, fall, lightning, summoning circle) with an original anime-style hero
- Reincarnation loading animation
- Random rebirth form: human, slime, dragon, spider, vending machine
- Life loop with energy bar: train, hunt, dungeon, rest, next year
- Pokemon-style turn-based battles (attack, skill, guard, run) with original monsters
- Evolution at level 10 with two paths per form, level resets to 1, plus a transformation scene
- Familiars gathered from battles
- Isekai shop, travel between worlds, bonds with goddesses and an elf knight
- Rebirth after death with a small legacy power bonus
- Code-drawn SVG creature sprites instead of emoji
- Autosave on the phone

## Planned next
- Battle animations: slashes, fireballs, screen shake
- More forms, evolution paths and abilities, more worlds and storylines
- Co-op: play with a friend using a room code (needs a free real-time service, check limits first)
